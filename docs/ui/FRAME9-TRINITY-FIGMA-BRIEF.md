## Mandatory visual states
Default, selected, focused, loading, empty, error, permission-denied, synthetic-data, disputed, reviewer-required, dry-run and rollback.

## Design principle
Every consequential value must answer “what is the source, what is its state, who can change it, and what happens next?” Use reusable components, auto layout and semantic component names. Never use a full-screen screenshot as the design deliverable.
